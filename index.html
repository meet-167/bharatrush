HOW TO PLAY

▲
JUMP: Press Arrow Up to jump over potholes, open manholes, and low barriers.
▼
SLIDE: Press Arrow Down to slide under street signs or clear hurdles.
◀ / ▶
MOVE LANES: Press Left/Right arrows to dodge vehicles like auto-rickshaws, bikes, and crazy rash drivers.
🧹
GARBAGE CLEANUP: Run over small garbage items (banana peels, plastic bottles, paper bags) on the street to earn extra score points and clean India!
